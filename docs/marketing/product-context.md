# YorkSims.com — Product Marketing Context

> Master context document. Every marketing, SEO, and copy artifact reads from this file. Update here first, then regenerate downstream assets.

## 1. What it is

**YorkSims.com** is a premium subscription learning + execution platform that documents York Sims building 10 different businesses/technologies in public. Instead of pre-recorded courses about theory, subscribers get live builds, the actual repos, the actual legal docs, the actual receipts.

**Product tagline:** *Teaching Execution, Not Theory.*
**Hero promise:** *Stop Learning. Start Building.*

## 2. The founder story (unique leverage)

- D1 college basketball → played pro overseas (Ankara, Turkey) → returned to build tech companies
- Now ships across **10 verticals simultaneously** through Caipher AI LLC
- Built autonomous AI agents (MoltBot, Otto) that run the back office
- Public GitHub footprint: 15+ repos, real production code
- Angle: "the discipline athletes learn is the biggest unfair advantage in business"

This is the single most defensible positioning asset. Every piece of marketing should reinforce it: **builder who actually ships, across domains most educators never touch (hardware RTL, land development, XRPL, etc.).**

## 3. ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)

### Primary ICP — "The Frustrated Builder" (80% of target)
- **Who:** 22–38 year old technical or semi-technical person
- **State:** Has consumed hundreds of hours of YouTube / Udemy / Twitter threads
- **Pain:** Knows a lot, ships very little. Has 5 half-built side projects. Tired of tutorials that stop at "Hello World"
- **Belief:** "I don't need more theory, I need to see someone actually ship something end-to-end"
- **Budget:** Will pay $29/mo for high-signal content without blinking; $499/mo needs an ROI story
- **Channels:** GitHub, Hacker News, Indie Hackers, X/Twitter builder community, specific YouTube channels
- **Trigger moments:** Launching a side project, quitting a job, starting an LLC, raising a pre-seed

### Secondary ICP — "The Crossover Athlete" (15%)
- **Who:** Former/current D1 or pro athletes transitioning into business
- **Pain:** Feels isolated — MBAs don't get them, founder Twitter doesn't get them
- **Hook:** "From D1 Basketball to Ankara to Building a Tech Company" — the founder story IS the lead magnet
- **Budget:** Over-indexes on the $499 Builder tier for the coaching access

### Tertiary ICP — "The Polymath" (5%)
- **Who:** Already running 1+ business, wants exposure to adjacent verticals (semiconductor, real estate, blockchain)
- **Pain:** Can't find trusted sources across domains — has to context-switch between siloed experts
- **Hook:** The 10-vertical coverage. No one else offers this breadth with depth
- **Budget:** $499/mo Builder tier is priced for them

## 4. Positioning

**Category frame:** "Execution platform" — not a course, not a bootcamp, not a newsletter. A *subscription to watch a builder build, with the receipts.*

**Primary competitive frame:**
| | Traditional courses | Newsletters | YorkSims |
|---|---|---|---|
| Content type | Pre-recorded theory | Text essays | Live builds + repos + contracts |
| Depth | Intro to one topic | Surface takes | 10 verticals, all end-to-end |
| Artifacts | Slides | None | Running code, LLC docs, templates |
| Feedback | None | Email reply | Monthly Q&A + community + coaching |
| Price anchor | $500–$2,000 one-time | $10–$30/mo | $29 / $499 per month |

**Messaging pillars** (every piece of copy must hit at least one):
1. **Show the work** — "Every post is a breakdown of something actually built. Code, receipts, decisions."
2. **Breadth + depth** — "10 verticals from a builder who ships in all of them."
3. **Execution > theory** — "No motivation. No fluff. No 'just ship it' platitudes."
4. **Unfair advantage** — the athlete→founder arc, the MoltBot/Otto automation stack.

## 6. Value props (ordered by conversion weight)

1. **You see the actual code.** Every session includes the repo.
2. **Ten verticals one builder.** Impossible breadth from one person with the receipts.
3. **Real contracts and legal docs.** LLC operating agreements, client contracts, NDAs — not templates from Google.
4. **Monthly live Q&A.** You can actually ask the builder.
5. **Gamified XP system.** Making progress is visible, not just vibes.
6. **Direct access at the Builder tier.** Email, coaching, priority.

## 9. Voice & tone

- **Direct, confident, no-bullshit.** "Stop learning. Start building." is the voice in one sentence.
- **Grounded in evidence.** Every claim has a repo, a contract, a number.
- **No hype words.** Avoid: "unleash," "game-changing," "revolutionary," "10x," "secret."
- **Short, punchy sentences.** Especially in headers. See blog posts for reference.
- **Athlete energy** — disciplined, not rah-rah. "The system is never 'done.' But it is good enough."
- **Uses first person "I" when York is the voice.** Uses "you" when addressing the reader.

## 10. Do / Don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Show a GitHub link | Show a stock hero illustration |
| Quote specific numbers | Say "thousands" or "many" |
| Name a real product | Use "our solution" |
| Use the founder's athlete story | Bury the founder story |
| Link to the actual repo | Link to a marketing landing page |
| Say "I built this" | Say "we helped X achieve Y" |
| Keep the dark + red aesthetic | Use AI-slop gradients |

## 11. Key metrics (to track once analytics are live)

- **Activation:** Free → Pro signup within 14 days
- **Engagement:** % of Pro subs who complete >1 portal item per week
- **Retention:** M2 retention on Pro ($29/mo)
- **Upgrade:** Pro → Builder conversion rate
- **Blog → Signup:** visits to /blog → Pro signups within 7 days
- **XP velocity:** average XP earned per week per subscriber (proxy for product-market fit)
