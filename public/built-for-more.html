By York Sims, Jr.

Built For More

Why every athlete's career ends, and how to build an identity that doesn't.

About 77,000 wordsISBN 978-0-557-73336-1PDF and paperback

Every athletic career ends. The kid who gets cut from JV and the Hall of Famer with a statue outside the stadium have that in common. There is always a last day.

I walk through every level of the funnel, from the first jersey to the pros, with the stories of real players who lived it, some of them hard to read. Then I show you what the people who came out whole did differently, and how to start doing it this season.

I'm not going to tell you to quit, and I'm not going to lie to you about the odds.

“Your past is not a prediction. It’s a preparation.”

From the introduction

Who it's for

  • Athletes 16 to 30 in any sport, whether you're still playing or already done.
