Who are you when the game is gone?

Built For More is for athletes who love their sport, and need a future beyond it. Through stories from high school to the professional ranks, York Sims explores what happens when the identity you’ve built around the game no longer fits.

Drawing on his own experience after basketball, he asks what athletes, parents, and coaches can do now to prepare for that transition. The book closes with a practical playbook for carrying the discipline, purpose, and ambition of sport into what comes next.

Why I wrote this

I played junior college ball at Panola College and Northern Oklahoma, one season of Division I at Central Arkansas, my senior year at Chico State, and then a brief stint in Turkey. When it ended, nobody had warned me what came next. That's why I wrote Built For More.

From the introduction

“Almost every adult in an athlete’s life asks some version of the same questions. What did you run? What are you benching? Who’s recruiting you? Where’d you commit? How’s the knee?

An athlete can go from age eight to age 28 answering questions every single day and never once hear the only one that is guaranteed to matter: Who are you when the game is gone?”
