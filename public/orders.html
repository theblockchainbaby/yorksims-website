Orders and delivery

PDFs

Your download link shows up on the screen right after checkout, and a copy comes to your email. If the link doesn't work or the file won't open, write me at hello@yorksims.com and I'll send you the PDF myself.

Print copies

Paperbacks ship to US addresses. Shipping is figured from your address and shown at checkout before you pay.

Every paperback order ships with tracking. You get the tracking number by email when it ships.

Refunds

PDFs can't be refunded once they're downloaded. If a file won't open, I'll fix it.

Damaged or misprinted books

If your book shows up damaged or printed wrong, email me a photo within 30 days of delivery and I'll send you a new copy at no charge.

Team orders

Bulk orders have their own price. I'll confirm it by email before you pay. See the teams page.
