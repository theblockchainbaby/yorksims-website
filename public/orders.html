Orders and delivery

PDFs

Your download link shows up on the screen right after checkout. Save the file somewhere you'll find it. If the link doesn't work or the file won't open, write me at york@yorksims.com with your receipt and I'll send you the PDF myself.

Print copies

Paperbacks ship from Folsom, California, to US addresses. Shipping is figured from your address and shown at checkout before you pay.

I ship within two business days of your order. Most US orders arrive in 2 to 5 days after that. You get a tracking number by email when it goes out.

Refunds

PDFs can't be refunded once they're downloaded. If a file won't open, I'll fix it.

Damaged or misprinted books

If your book shows up damaged or printed wrong, email me a photo within 30 days of delivery and I'll send you a new copy at no charge.

Team orders

Bulk orders have their own price. I'll confirm it by email before you pay. See the teams page.
