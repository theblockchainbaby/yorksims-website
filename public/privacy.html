Privacy

Last updated November 8, 2026

This site sells my books. I collect as little as I can and I don't sell or rent anything about you. Here is the whole picture.

Who runs the site

yorksims.com is run by Caipher AI LLC, a Wyoming company, which is me, York Sims, Jr. Write to york@yorksims.com with any question about this page.

What I collect

Orders. Checkout runs on Stripe. Stripe collects your card and, for paperbacks, your shipping address. I see your name, email, what you bought and the shipping address. I never see your full card number.

Forms. If you ask for the free introduction, join the memoir list or request a team order, I keep the email and whatever else you typed in the form so I can reply.

Server logs. The hosting company keeps standard logs, like IP address and the page requested, for a short time to keep the site running.

There are no accounts, no passwords and no advertising trackers on this site.

How I use it

To deliver what you bought, answer what you asked and, if you joined the memoir list, send you one email when the memoir is out. That's it. No newsletters you didn't ask for.

Who else sees it

Stripe for payments, the print shop that makes and ships paperbacks (name and address only), and the companies that host the site and store form entries. None of them may use your information for their own marketing.

Your choices

Email york@yorksims.com and I will delete your form entries or take you off the memoir list. Order records stay as long as tax and accounting rules require.

Children

The books are written for athletes 16 and up, but the site is not meant to collect information from anyone under 13. If a child has given me an email, tell me and I'll remove it.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it.
