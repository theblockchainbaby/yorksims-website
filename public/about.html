York Sims, Jr.

York Sims, Jr. was born in Clarksville, Arkansas, in 1986. He spent the first semester of his senior year at Millington Central outside Memphis, then moved back to Clarksville to graduate with the class of 2005. In eighth grade and again in eleventh, he lived with his high school's athletic director and dean of students.

He started college at Panola College in Texas in 2005-06, then moved to Northern Oklahoma College in Enid for 2006-07, where he set the school's single-season records for scoring and steals, with an NJCAA Player of the Week along the way.

In 2007 he signed with the University of Central Arkansas and played one season of NCAA Division I in the Southland Conference. He played guard at 6-2, started 26 of 30 games and averaged 12.4 points. The season opened at Rupp Arena against Kentucky on November 6, 2007. His career high was 24 points against the University of Texas at San Antonio.

He finished as a senior at Chico State in 2008-09. In 2009 he flew to Ankara, Turkey, for a pro tryout with Orman Spor.

After basketball he founded a company, Elite Eighth, and sold it in 2024. He builds software and works in logistics operations. He lives in Folsom, California, with his wife and daughter.
