The Built For More Award

$500 for books, for a junior college sophomore who's already building a life beyond sports.

I played junior college ball before Division I, and I earned my degree at Northern Oklahoma College. Most juco athletes know the game ends soon. This award is for the ones doing something about it now.

Three awards, $500 each

  • One for an athlete at Northern Oklahoma College.
  • One for an athlete at Folsom Lake College.
  • One shared award for athletes at American River College, Sacramento City College, Cosumnes River College and Sierra College.

Who can apply

Sophomore athletes on a team roster at one of those colleges, any sport.

What wins

The best plan for life after sports, and proof you're already working on it. Classes toward a major, a certification, an internship, a job, a business you started. A plan you've only written down doesn't win. A plan you've started does.

How to apply

Fill out the form. Tell me your plan and the steps you've already taken, and give me a coach, counselor or advisor who can confirm them. You can paste a link to a short video or a document instead of typing it all out.

Dates

Applications close March 1, 2027. Winners are announced during March Madness. The award goes to the college for the winner's books.

I read every application myself. Your information is only used for this award.
